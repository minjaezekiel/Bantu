Hand-written macro-F1 = **0.513**, measured at the thresholds calibrated on the held-out set — never on this one.

**macro-F1 = 0.607** at the calibrated thresholds, 0.603 at a flat 0.5. Mean held-out BCE = 0.818.

Thresholds are swept on this held-out split for the best F2 (recall weighted twice as heavily as precision) subject to a per-label precision floor, and are stored in `toxicity.btw` alongside the weights.

The split is over GENERATORS, not rows: about a fifth of the templates and a fifth of each slot's values are held out, so a held-out sentence is a phrasing the model never saw, filled with words it never saw in that role. An earlier version split rows at random and reported a macro-F1 of 1.000 — that number measured memorisation of 89 sentence patterns, nothing else.

These numbers describe this corpus. They are a floor, not a claim about real reported content — see `docs/lugha-architecture.md` section 6.
